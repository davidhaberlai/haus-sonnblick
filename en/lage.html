Home/Location & Directions/Location

On the sunny side of Bad Kleinkirchheim.

On the southern slope, sunny and central: thermal spa, gondola and hiking trails are quickly reached from the house.

The house enjoys a sunny, central location, close to the Kaiserburg gondola and about 600 metres from the Thermal-Römerbad. In winter the ski shuttle stops right below the house and the cross-country trail is just a few minutes’ walk away. In summer you step straight into the hiking area of the Nock Mountains Biosphere Park.

  • Ski shuttle stopright below the house
  • Bad Kleinkirchheim golf courseapprox. 500 m
  • Thermal-Römerbadapprox. 600 m
  • Cross-country ski traila few minutes’ walk
  • Kaiserburg gondola (valley station)approx. 1 km
